# Before the Sun Rises — ፀሐይ ሳትወጣ

I want to make a short film about an Ethiopian mother who wakes up early in the morning to prepare injera for her family. While her son is still sleeping, she prepares the batter, heats the mitad and starts baking.

When her son wakes up, he joins her for breakfast. Before eating, he offers his mother the first piece of injera. It is a small gesture, but it shows that he is beginning to understand the love behind her everyday work.

I want to tell this story because injera is more than food in Ethiopian culture. It brings families together and reminds us of the people whose efforts we sometimes take for granted.

My intended audience is Ethiopian families, young Ethiopians and people living abroad who miss the feeling of being at home. I want them to remember their mothers, appreciate their everyday sacrifices and feel connected to their culture.

The film will have a warm, realistic Ethiopian atmosphere, with traditional household objects, natural morning light and simple camera movements. The narration will be in Amharic, accompanied by English subtitles and original instrumental music inspired by the krar, masenqo and washint.

The film will be approximately 75 seconds long and available in both vertical and horizontal formats. I will use fictional characters rather than reproduce the face or voice of a real person.

Before generating the final visuals, I will share the narration with at least three people from my intended audience and use their feedback to improve the story. I will repeat this process with the near-final film.
